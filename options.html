Data & privacy

PennyPilot runs entirely on your device. No account, no server, no tracking. Your ZIP is resolved locally to show city/county data and never leaves the browser.

Data sources

State minimum wages & tax deadlines: U.S. DOL, IRS, state Departments of Revenue (retrieved 2026-08). City wages & taxes: official city publications (Seattle OLS, SF OLSE, DC DOES, Denver Auditor, Minneapolis, CA DIR, NYC/Phila. depts). Benefits: USDA, IRS, HealthCare.gov. Verify any figure with the cited source before relying on it.
